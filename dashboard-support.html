FAQ Quick Links

How long do transfers take?

Direct account transfers are processed instantly within Payvexis. Outer transfers take 1-3 business days.

Can I unfreeze a card?

Yes, you can freeze or unfreeze your active Payvexis debit cards instantly at any time from the Cards tab.

What if my card is blocked?

If your card or account was blocked due to transaction verification, please open a "Security Report" ticket on the left.
